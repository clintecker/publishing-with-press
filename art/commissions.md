## Logomark

The imprint device of LGTM Publishing, per the press-logomark skill: a
printer's mark, single ink, contained silhouette, the initials named
verbatim. It must read at 0.5 in wide in one ink.

```
A printer's mark for a small publishing house, in the tradition of
nineteenth-century printers' devices: a circular roundel made of two plain
concentric rules, and inside it a common cuttlefish (Sepia officinalis)
seen from the side, facing left, drawn as a simple engraved silhouette with
a few bold hatching lines, its arms gathered forward. Across the lower
part of the roundel, in a plain horizontal band between two thin rules, the
four capital letters LGTM in a classical roman serif, letter for letter,
nothing else, no quotation marks. Single black ink on a fully transparent
background, bold enough to read when printed half an inch wide: few fine
lines, no gray washes, no gradients, no shadow, no other text, no frame
beyond the roundel.
```
